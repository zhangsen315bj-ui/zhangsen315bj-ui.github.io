youtube-publisher

youtube-publisher is a private, single-owner tool that uploads videos to the owner's own YouTube channels through the official YouTube Data API v3.

What it does

  • Uploads videos, titles, descriptions and thumbnails that the owner has prepared and approved.
  • Reads back the status of those uploads on the owner's channels.

Who can use it

Only the channel owner. It is not offered to the public and no other users sign in to it.
